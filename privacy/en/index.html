Legal / Privacy

We don't want your data. We built it that way.

This policy covers every app published by Akavjah Creations, including Mad Orbit Dash. It's short on purpose — there isn't much to disclose when nothing leaves your device.

Last updated — August 11, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, or share any personal data, and we never ask you to create an account or sign in.

Your scores and game progress are saved locally on your device only. They're never transmitted to us or to anyone else — if you delete the app, they're gone.

Third-party services

None. Our apps don't use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind. There's no ad network watching how you play, and no analytics package phoning home.

  • No analytics or telemetry
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No third-party crash or usage tracking

If an app offers a purchase, it's handled entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never see or store your payment information.

Children's privacy

Because we don't collect data from anyone, we don't collect data from children either. Our apps are safe for players of any age to use in that respect, in line with COPPA and similar child-privacy regulations.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we'll update the date above and post the new version here. Given our apps collect nothing today, we don't expect that to change — but if it ever did, you'd hear about it before it happened, not after.

Questions

If anything here is unclear, or you just want to double-check something before trusting us with a download, reach out directly.
